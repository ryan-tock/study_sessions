Course Links

Strong link — Courses that are the same class (e.g. MATH213 Calc 3 and MATH223 Calc 3 Honors). They share the same test, so study sessions are combined into one with students and tutors from both courses.

Weak link — Courses that cover related material but have separate tests (e.g. EENG281 Intro to Electrical Circuits and EENG282 Intro to Electronic Circuits). Tutors for one are shown as available for the other, but study sessions and student lists stay separate.
